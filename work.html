MeetReal

IAT 334: Interface Design

Project Duration

June 2024 - August 2024 (6 weeks)

My Role

UI/UX Research, Interface Design

Context of Problem

MeetReal is a dating app designed to improve the challenge that the dating app users encounter difficulty in creating a connection from the dating app to an in-person date, due to various date preferences for individuals or safety issues about meeting strangers in real life.

Solution

In order to solve the problems, MeetReal Date Setup includes three different features. First, it allows the users to set their date preferences to find the ideal date place. Secondly, it helps users set up their safety contact, and lastly, it receives users' feedback about their experiences.

I focused on...

I focused on one of the core functions of dating setup such as setting up the date preferences tab, its visual design of toggle buttons for setting the date preferences tab.

Redesign Analysis

The previous design of the toggle switches looked too similar to the style of the main buttons. It was highlighted by the TA, who provided feedback that this similar design might lead users mistakenly to think that clicking the toggle switches would act, rather than simply switching states.

Outcome

he re-design for setting the preferences tab was successful, leading to a better interface design as a result. As emojis have been added, it could make the users understand what it means better and bring them a more friendly context. As the toggle switches differ from the main buttons, it does not break the heuristic principle of 'Recognition rather than recall' by its distinct style to help users identify their function. Also, keeping the original design for the confirm button at the bottom keeps the heuristic rule of ‘Consistency and Standards.'

Prototype

Reflection

From this experience, I have learned that understanding the user's perspective is crucial for improving interface design. Keep in structure with chosen style would be one of the important thing to build a consistent and effective design.
